\opening{Dear Editors,}

Please consider my manuscript, “Uniform-Price Equilibria and Welfare with Switching Costs: A Reassessment of Gehrig, Shy, and Stenbacka (2012),” for publication as a Research Paper in \emph{Information Economics and Policy}.

The paper revisits an established history-based price-discrimination benchmark with switching costs. Starting from primitive consumer choice, it derives the complete pure-strategy uniform-price correspondence and identifies an exact inherited-share threshold that is missing from the global equilibrium claim in the post-referee accepted manuscript of Gehrig, Shy, and Stenbacka (2012). The correction sharply narrows the parameter set on which uniform and history-based pricing can be compared as pure equilibria.

The paper also reconstructs consumer surplus and welfare over the actual clipped allocation. The weak-dominance consumer-surplus reversal reported in the accepted manuscript is not reproduced; on the corrected pure-equilibrium overlap, the smaller firm earns less under history-based pricing while the qualitative welfare ranking in favor of uniform pricing survives. Under strong dominance, the paper separates price-selection-sensitive consumer surplus from selection-invariant social welfare.

I believe the manuscript fits \emph{Information Economics and Policy} because it concerns how customer-history information changes firms' pricing incentives and welfare, and because the journal has recently published closely related theoretical work on behavior-based pricing, switching costs, consumer recognition, and asymmetric market shares. The contribution is deliberately model-specific: the paper does not claim a generic nonexistence theorem and does not characterize mixed pricing below the pure-existence threshold.

The analysis is supported by an exact-rational replication suite, independent symbolic reconstruction, deterministic figure generation, and Lean 4/mathlib checks of selected proof-critical algebra. No empirical dataset is used. Reproducibility materials are available in the accompanying repository.

This manuscript is original, has not been published in this form, and is not under consideration by another journal. I am the sole author and take full responsibility for the manuscript. The paper includes declarations of funding, competing interests, data/code availability, and the use of generative AI and AI-assisted technologies.

Thank you for considering the manuscript.
