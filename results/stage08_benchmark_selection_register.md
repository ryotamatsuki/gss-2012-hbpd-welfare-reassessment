# Benchmark, Selection, and Welfare Register

## 1. Uniform-pricing benchmark

The canonical uniform benchmark is the **pure-strategy Nash equilibrium of the original GSS uniform-price game**, only where it exists.

It exists iff:

- \(s=0\); or
- \(0<s<1\) and \(x_0\ge x_H(s)\).

Below \(x_H\), the accepted Eq. (12) vector is a counterfactual profile, not an equilibrium benchmark.

## 2. Weak-HBP benchmark

Use the accepted weak-HBP equilibrium branch only on its certified source domain.

Equilibrium comparisons to uniform pricing require simultaneous satisfaction of the weak-HBP domain and the corrected pure-uniform domain.

## 3. Strong-HBP benchmark and selection

If below-cost poaching prices are admitted, strong HBP contains a payoff-equivalent zero-sales family.

For CS/profit comparisons, the canonical manuscript benchmark is the **source/nonnegative-margin member \(q_A=c\)**.

This is a selection convention and must be disclosed.

For social welfare, all members of the family induce the same allocation and real resource cost, so the welfare comparison is selection-invariant.

## 4. Welfare definition

Social welfare is
\[
W=CS+\pi_A+\pi_B.
\]

With full coverage and common marginal cost, prices are transfers. Welfare differences are therefore determined by real transport and switching costs.

The manuscript does **not** use or claim:

- first best;
- constrained first best;
- second best;
- planner optimum.

Accordingly, no planner benchmark is part of the frozen theory.

## 5. Multiplicity/nonexistence taxonomy

- uniform pure equilibrium: singleton where it exists;
- uniform pure equilibrium below \(x_H\): empty;
- uniform mixed equilibrium below \(x_H\): unresolved/out of scope;
- strong-HBP unrestricted-price vector for (x_0<1): potentially multiple through the zero-sales family;
- strong-HBP full price vector at (x_0=1): nonunique because one history segment is empty;
- strong-HBP allocation/social welfare across the nondegenerate family and endpoint unused-price multiplicity: invariant;
- strong-HBP CS/profits for (x_0<1): selection-dependent; endpoint CS gap is zero under the empty-segment limit.

## 6. Terminology rule

Use:

- “pure-strategy equilibrium” where appropriate;
- “counterfactual Eq. (12) profile” outside the pure-existence region;
- “source/nonnegative-margin strong-HBP selection” for CS/profit claims;
- “selection-invariant welfare” where proven.

Do not use selection-free wording where the benchmark is not selection-free.
