# Stage 3 — Paper architecture decision

**Verdict:** GO  
**Selected route:** B — corrected pure-equilibrium reassessment  
**Workflow:** canonical v2.4  
**Decision date:** 2026-09-23

## Research question

What is the complete pure-strategy uniform-price equilibrium correspondence in the inherited-history switching-cost model used by Gehrig, Shy, and Stenbacka (2012), and how does that corrected equilibrium domain alter the accepted manuscript's market-share, profit, consumer-surplus, and welfare comparisons?

## Candidate A — minimal correction

Content:

* one exact counterexample to accepted-version Eq. (12);
* correction of the accepted-version Eq. (15) algebra.

Assessment: **reject as final architecture**. It establishes that two displayed claims require correction but leaves unanswered the economically central question: which parameters actually support the source uniform-price profile as a global pure Nash equilibrium?

## Candidate B — corrected pure-equilibrium reassessment

Content:

* primitive consumer choice for both purchase histories;
* full clipped uniform demand over all five price-difference regimes;
* complete pure-strategy best-response/equilibrium correspondence;
* exact necessary-and-sufficient domain for the accepted-version Eq. (12) profile;
* equality/multiplicity analysis;
* pure-strategy nonexistence outside that domain;
* independent branch-correct CS integration;
* corrected market-share/profit/welfare implications;
* explicit restriction of equilibrium welfare comparisons to the pure-existence domain.

Assessment: **selected**. This is the smallest architecture that resolves the global-equilibrium issue rather than merely exhibiting it.

## Candidate C — extended mixed/generalized model

Possible additions:

* characterize mixed pricing when the uniform game has no pure equilibrium;
* generalize the inherited installed base or information structure.

Assessment: **defer / outside current correction scope**. Neither extension is needed to correct the accepted manuscript's pure “unique Nash–Bertrand equilibrium” claim. Adding them now would change the research question and create material research creep.

## Mixed-strategy contract

The paper will characterize the **complete pure-strategy price correspondence**. If the Stage-4 theorem survives certification, the region below the threshold will be described only as a region with **no pure-strategy price equilibrium**. The paper will not claim general Nash nonexistence and will not assign equilibrium CS/profit/welfare there without a mixed-equilibrium solution.

## Contribution classification

The project is best classified as a **mathematical reassessment/correction with a new exact source-specific equilibrium-domain theorem**. It is not marketed as a general theorem for all switching-cost Bertrand games.

## Next-stage contract

Proceed to Stage 4/4A with architecture B. Mixed pricing may be reopened only if later evidence shows it is indispensable for a claim the paper must make; otherwise preserve it as an explicit extension.
