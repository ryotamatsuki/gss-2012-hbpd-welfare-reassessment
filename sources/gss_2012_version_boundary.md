## Publication-facing wording rule

Because a lawful accepted manuscript is available but the VOR mathematical body is not directly inspected:

* permitted: “the accepted manuscript states…”, “the post-referee manuscript gives…”, “the accepted-version Eq. (12)…”, or explicitly version-qualified descriptions;
* prohibited until VOR inspection: “the published Eq. (12) is wrong”, “the Version of Record contains…”, or any assertion that depends on exact typeset VOR mathematics.

If a lawful VOR copy is later obtained, compare the exact disputed lines before upgrading the wording.
