# Claim Boundary

## Allowed publication-development claims
- Complete the global uniform-price equilibrium correspondence and state corrected existence/region results.
- Recompute consumer surplus, profits, and welfare using the corrected equilibrium branches.
- Distinguish the equilibrium correction from the independent Eq. (15) integration correction.

## Claims currently prohibited
- Do not claim that the entire HBP equilibrium is invalid; the master audit reproduced the HBP branches conditionally.
- Do not attribute any equation-level error to the final VOR until the VOR body is directly checked.

## General boundary
Until Stage 0 and Stage 4 pass, use language such as “the transferred audit indicates,” “candidate correction,” or “to be independently re-verified.” Do not use “proved,” “corrected theorem,” “published result is false,” or equivalent publication-facing language except when referring explicitly to the historical master-audit finding rather than this repository’s frozen result.
